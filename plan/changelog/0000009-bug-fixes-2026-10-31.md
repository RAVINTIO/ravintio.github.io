# Changelog (0000009-bug-fixes, 31 Oct 2026)

**Feature:** Bug fixes for copy, mobile nav, and dependencies
**Version:** 0.4.1
**PR:** [#24](https://github.com/RAVINTIO/ravintio.github.io/pull/24)

## What changed

- `what-is-ravintio` now opens with what RAVINTIO does and a "Why use RAVINTIO" list of ten benefits. The name section and letter table are gone. Three shared principles remain.
- `zero-overview` leads with "Why use Zero" and adds "How Zero keeps solutions simple" and "When Zero fits". "Terms" moves to the end.
- `pipeline` P2 says that each improvement cycle looks for a simpler solution.
- `first-run` step 2 creates `plan/current/` before the copy, so the command works on a fresh setup.
- Smaller copy fixes cover `install-zero`, `first-run`, `six-tools`, and the homepage subtitle.
- On screens 900 px wide or less, the page list collapses into a disclosure above the content. It names the current page.
- `mermaid` moves to 12.2.0 and the lockfile updates `vite` to 7.3.7. `npm audit` reports 0 vulnerabilities.
- Five new test files bring the suite to 38 tests.

## Why

The 0.4.0 site explained RAVINTIO from the inside out. A developer deciding whether to adopt it met the name and the internals before the reasons. The first-run step 2 command failed on a fresh setup. On a phone, ten nav links came before the content. The build chain carried 16 known advisories.

## Deviations

- `mermaid` 12 is a major bump. The human approved it at a P2 stop after a new `katex` advisory appeared.
- The 0.4.0 "eight principles" test was retired, because the letter table is gone.
- The phrase "as simple as the problem allows" is exempt from the `simple` style check.
- P2 ran each unit inline and used one improvement cycle for all requirements.
- After ship, at the human's request, three question headings gained a question mark: "What is RAVINTIO?", "Why use RAVINTIO?", and "Why use Zero?". The nav title and homepage button match. Anchors are unchanged.
- After ship, at the human's request, the tool names in the `six-tools` "At a glance" table break before the second word. A fixed `6rem` span keeps the column the same width at every window size.
- After ship, at the human's request, the Unbound status label became "In design" in the table and in its section. The Area column already says "Experimental research".

## Validation results

- 38 of 38 tests pass. Typecheck and build pass.
- Browser checks at 360 by 740 px and 1280 px verified all 13 criteria.
- No critical, high, or medium security finding is open.

## Skipped phases

None
